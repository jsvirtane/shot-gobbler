shots-list: The view toggle gains a third "Stats" option, and a compact per-team summary line ("ARS 6 sh 4 OT 2 G 1.36 xG | CHE 5 sh …") appears above the list.
shots-stats: The new shots Stats view shows a per-team Totals table (shots, on target, goals, on-target %, conversion, xG, xG per shot, goals − xG) and a breakdown by body part and shot type.
touches-stats: The touches Stats view shows totals tiles (actions, pass completion, defensive won), a per-action success table and zone heatmaps for all and defensive actions.
chains-stats: The pass-chains Stats view shows chain totals (actions per chain, reached final third, ended in a shot), how chains ended, and a start-third to end-third table.
