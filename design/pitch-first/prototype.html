Shot Gobbler · design mockup v2

Pitch-first tracking

The pitch fills the phone. Every event is entered on a ring that opens where you tap. Lists, stats and filters wait in review mode.

Try it

  • Tap the pitch. The half you tap picks the team; tap the team in the middle of the ring to switch it.
  • Tap Saved for a one-tap shot. Tap Head, then Goal, for a headed goal.
  • Tap outside the ring to cancel.
  • Tap an existing marker to edit, move or delete it.
  • Bottom bar: tracker, clock, half pitch, undo, leave focus.

What changed in v2

  • Rings are split into labelled sections
  • Team switch in the middle of the shot ring
  • Icons match the pitch markers

Not in this mockup

  • Saving, matches, stats and export. Data resets on reload.
