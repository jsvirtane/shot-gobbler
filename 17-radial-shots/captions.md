tap-pitch: Tapping the pitch in focus mode no longer opens the full-screen New Shot form; a ring opens at the tap with one-tap results (Goal/Saved/Blocked/Miss), Head/Set piece/Penalty toggles, Shirt # and the team badge in the centre.
half-pitch: The half pitch is flipped so the goal is at the bottom (nearest the thumb), and the bar gains a team toggle (Home) for whose shots are being recorded.
head-toggle: Turning on Head ticks the segment blue and updates the centre summary to "Head · Open" before a result is picked.
edit-ring: Tapping an existing shot opens the edit ring with its current result outlined plus Move, Minute and Delete.
